The Break and Build Lab

Math Explorer Club

Interactive tools for understanding the four operations: what adding, taking away, multiplying and dividing mean, and why the methods we use for them work.

Operations: Add, Subtract, Multiply, Divide

  1. Beans in Two Boxes Move beans one at a time: one box goes up, the other goes down, and the total stays the same. Then take away, and make two boxes equal to find the difference.
  2. Jumps on the Number Line Break a jump into smaller jumps you choose, or count up to subtract. Then equal jumps: multiplication, and division as repeated subtraction with a remainder.
